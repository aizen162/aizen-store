Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Aizen Store ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Order information: your name, phone number, address and city, provided when you place an order via WhatsApp.
  • Contact information: your name, email and message when you use our contact or newsletter forms.
  • Technical data: basic analytics such as pages visited and device type, collected via cookies or analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, ship and support your orders.
  • To deliver digital products to your email.
  • To respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To send newsletters only if you explicitly subscribed (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • To improve our website, products and content.

3. Cookies & Advertising

We may use cookies to remember your cart and to understand site usage. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed to fulfil orders (e.g. courier companies for delivery) or when required by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at support@aizenstore.pk.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via the contact page.
